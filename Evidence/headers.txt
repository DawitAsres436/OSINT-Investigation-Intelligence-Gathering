- HSTS is enabled with max-age=63072000.
- HTTP/3 availability is advertised through the Alt-Svc header.
- X-Vercel-ID indicates Vercel infrastructure is present behind the
  observed HTTP service.
- CF-Cache-Status is DYNAMIC.
- No Content-Security-Policy header was observed in this response.
- No X-Frame-Options header was observed in this response.
- No X-Content-Type-Options header was observed in this response.
- No Referrer-Policy header was observed in this response.
- No Permissions-Policy header was observed in this response.

Important:
The absence of a header in this response does not by itself establish
a security vulnerability. The test was performed against the initial
HTTP 307 response from the bare domain.
